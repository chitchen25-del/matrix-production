About Clare Kelly

Practitioner Profile

Bridging Functional Medicine & Soft-Tissue Therapy

Clare Kelly is a leading functional health and soft-tissue practitioner based in Castletown, Isle of Man. With decades of clinical experience, Clare established The Natural Healing Clinic with a singular mission: to provide patients suffering from chronic pain, systemic inflammation, and deep-seated physical trauma with clear, sustainable results in a realistic timeframe.

Her clinical methodology is strictly evidence-informed, drawing from advanced training in Bowen Therapy, Manual Lymphatic Drainage (MLD), and Functional Medicine frameworks. Clare works closely alongside mainstream medical protocols, investigating the root causes of musculoskeletal disorders, autoimmune flare-ups, and nervous system dysregulation rather than merely masking symptoms.
